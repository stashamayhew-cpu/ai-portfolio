Context and approach

The challenge

Different business teams need support processes that fit their work, while enterprise applications require consistent troubleshooting, validation, and reliable service delivery.

My approach

I work with business stakeholders and technical partners to translate service needs into practical configurations, support models, and improvement plans.

Work and contributions

What I worked on

Finance workspace: intake, dashboards & routing

Configured category- and ERP-module-based request forms capturing business context, priority, error details, and supporting references. Built priority-based routing workflows and dashboard views that help Finance teams organize urgent requests, open items, and request status.

Legal workspace: service catalog & NDA intake

Configured a Legal service catalog with contract and agreement review, general legal requests, legal advice, and NDA requests. Designed NDA intake fields for request type, external party, turnaround needs, and sensitivity-related review questions.

Legal workflow automation

Created routing rules for general legal requests, NDA requests, contract reviews, and urgent submissions. Configured rules to direct incoming requests to designated groups based on request type and priority.

Application support & operations

Lead and coordinate L3/L4 application support, escalations, vendor follow-through, and technical issue investigation for Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM and Finance & Operations, SmartSimple, Microsoft 365, and other enterprise services.

Integrations, UAT & releases

Support integrations and enterprise system implementations through requirements discussions, troubleshooting, test coordination, UAT leadership, issue tracking, release validation, and business communication. This is not a claim of personally implementing Dynamics CRM or ERP.

Service leadership

Coordinate internal help desk operations and external support providers, review service needs, guide support processes, and develop knowledge resources to improve user experience.
