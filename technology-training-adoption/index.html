Technology Training & Adoption

Helping employees confidently use enterprise applications

Professional experience
Context and approach

The challenge

New systems and frequent technology changes create friction when employees cannot easily find guidance suited to their role or experience.

My approach

Built a centralized learning resource and developed approachable, role-specific training to support adoption across enterprise technologies.

Work and contributions

What I worked on

Technology Learning Central

Created a SharePoint-based technology learning hub with tips, registration information, knowledge resources, self-service links, and Microsoft learning materials.

Organization-wide training

Developed and delivered training and guidance across Microsoft 365, Copilot foundations, CRM, ERP support, intranet roles, and other enterprise systems.

Role-based learning

Produced step-by-step guides, knowledge-base content, webinars, and hands-on explanations for staff with different technical backgrounds.

Feedback and iteration

Used stakeholder questions, user feedback, and practical support experience to refine materials and reduce confusion after changes.

Technology

Platforms & methods

SharePoint · Microsoft 365 · Microsoft Teams · Copilot · Dynamics 365 · Knowledge management

Project outcome: Created reusable learning and support resources and delivered training programs. Attendance and adoption metrics are not asserted without supporting evidence.
